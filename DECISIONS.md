# Decisions

1. **Video-only labels.** Use `VIDEO_*` Neo4j labels and relationships so this prototype cannot merge entities with the team's other pipelines. Reconcile with Dhruv's schema before loading into a shared database.
2. **Model.** Use the small COCO-panoptic Mask2Former checkpoint for an internal demo until Aayush confirms a reusable team model. Its label IDs 0-79 denote things and 80+ denote stuff. The weights licence is unclear, so no weights redistribution.
3. **Sampling.** Analyze at approximately 2 fps and resize the longest side to 640 px to fit the available RTX 3070 Ti laptop GPU. Preserve source frame numbers for retrieval and trimming.
4. **Segments and tracks.** Ignore segments scored below 0.5. Associate consecutive same-class masks at IoU >= 0.2 and allow two missed samples. These are demo defaults, not tuned estimates; ID switches remain possible.
5. **Relations.** `near` means centroid separation <= 0.12 of frame diagonal. `touching` means masks meet after an 8 px dilation. `approaching` means normalized separation falls by >= 0.015 between observations. Merge across at most one missing sample and require two supporting samples to suppress one-frame noise. These are observable geometric proxies, not semantic proof of holding or intent.
6. **Snippets.** Add up to 2 s on either side, clip to source bounds, re-encode every source frame, and overlay the nearest sampled mask. The mask can drift between samples; the sidecar records the sampling step.
7. **Additional rules.** `on` needs mask contact near an object's top edge (8 px). `holding` needs contact and co-motion (>=0.005 diagonal per sample, <=0.025 difference). `lying_on` needs a person width/height ratio >=1.3 against a support class; `falling` additionally needs a downward centroid shift >=0.08 frame heights. `entering`/`leaving` require a region explicitly drawn in normalized coordinates; none is configured for the current clip. These conservative defaults are uncalibrated proxies and must be checked on validation scenes before interpretation.
8. **GPU jobs.** Cap CPU math libraries at four threads and stop analysis if the laptop GPU reaches 85 C; record temperature in the job log. Run only one model inference process at a time.
9. **MEVA source availability.** The fixed activity mapping was committed before evaluation. The first 50-clip selection included three annotated clips absent from the public S3 bucket; the selector now checks S3 availability before choosing a clip. `data/meva/selection_initial.json` and `unavailable_clips.json` preserve the reason for replacement. No evaluation labels or model scores were used to choose replacements.
10. **Rule confidence.** A frame relation's score is the lower model confidence of its two segments (the person score for a configured region). Interval confidence is the mean frame score times the fraction of sampled positions with rule support after gap merging. Neo4j edge confidence is the minimum of interval confidence and the mean scores of both endpoint tracks. This records extraction uncertainty; it is not calibrated event probability.
